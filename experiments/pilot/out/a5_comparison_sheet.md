## Pair 1

### Story A

Life at the colony tested its inhabitants' resilience. While Lidia "Stench" Schmitt managed local wildlife—shepherding a wandering pet named Papyrus and occasionally missing her marks on tortoises—the psychological toll mounted. Discontent over abysmal living quarters plunged Dillon Boyd into a nervous, wandering psychotic state. 

Amidst the domestic strain, underlying tensions briefly surfaced when Odette O'Neill raised a hand toward Stench, only to hesitate at the final second. Meanwhile, the frontier continued its unpredictable churn: a transport pod carrying a wounded medieval farm oaf from a hostile faction crashed nearby, leaving the strained colonists to weigh their next move.

### Story B

Following a sudden raider attack that left the nervous Dillon Boyd shaken, the colony's fragile stability began to fracture. Weary from poor living conditions in the awful barracks, Dillon succumbed to a psychotic wandering state. Meanwhile, tensions boiled over when Odette insulted Lidia "Stench" Schmitt, abruptly sparking a violent brawl between the two friends. Despite Odette throwing punches at Stench—who was already distracted by missed hunts and local wildlife encounters—the fight quickly dissolved as hesitation set in. Amidst the chaos, routine chores continued with Stench hauling supplies, while shared conversations about aiding the downtrodden offered brief glimmers of enduring camaraderie.

### Story A

Pushed to the brink by wretched living conditions, the weary civil servant Dillon Boyd succumbed to a psychotic break, wandering aimlessly through the settlement in a daze. 

Meanwhile, recovery and routine offered brief reprieves from the despair. The pyromaniac hunter Lidia Schmitt fully healed from her injuries, returning to her duties of herding livestock and hauling valuable silver. Yet, her aim proved fallible out in the wild.
