Odette O'Neill scanned the treeline for raiders as Lidia 'Stench' Schmitt healed from her wounds and fired at a turkey nearby. While the colony remained unaware, a wounded medieval laborer named Ackmann crashed in a hostile transport pod. Back inside, hunger frayed Odette’s temper until she turned on her friend Stench, following her with insults before the episode broke. Their quarrel turned physical: Odette lunged and stopped short, Stench threw a clumsy punch and stumbled, and Odette fell, defeated.
