The colony teetered on the edge of collapse as repeated warnings flashed over the comms: major break risks were mounting. The pressure finally shattered Dillon Boyd; driven mad by the squalor of the barracks, he descended into a psychotic wander, muttering through the halls. While Boyd unraveled, Lidia 'Stench' Schmitt—recently recovered from her own injuries—kept the routine turning. She herded the boar Papyrus into a new pen, missed a clean shot on a tortoise, and hauled a massive load of silver to secure the stockpiles. 

Beyond the perimeter, fate delivered an unexpected variable. A transport pod screamed down from orbit, crashing hard in the wilds. Its sole occupant, a medieval farmhand named Ackmann loyal to the hostile Alyona's Rockets, survived the impact only to lie broken and unconscious in the wreckage. Nearby, a flock of turkeys dropped inexplicably, felled by unseen sickness or poison, scattering easy meat across the grasslands.
