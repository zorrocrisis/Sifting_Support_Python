The colony’s perimeter teemed with timber wolves, a constant, unseen pressure on the palisade. Inside, tension snapped. Dillon Boyd, worn down by the squalor of the shared barracks, succumbed to a psychotic break, wandering the halls in a daze. While Lidia "Stench" Schmitt recovered from old wounds and tended to livestock—hauling silver and herding Papyrus, though a tortoise eluded her—fortune fell from the sky. A transport pod from Alyona's Rockets crashed nearby, depositing a medieval farmhand named Ackmann, badly wounded. He lay unconscious in the dirt, neutralized before he could raise a weapon, while turkeys dropped inexplicably around the crash site. The colony held its breath, waiting to see if the stranger would live or die.
