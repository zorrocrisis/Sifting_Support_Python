Life at the remote colony settled into a routine of survival as Lidia "Stench" Schmitt scouted for game, successfully bagging a wild turkey and tending to livestock while hauling newfound wealth to the stockpile. Beneath the surface, however, tensions brewed. Plagued by ravenous hunger and a deteriorating mood, an abrasive Odette O'Neill began relentlessly harassing Stench. The verbal abuse swiftly escalated into physical violence. In the ensuing altercation, Odette sustained a severe bruise and collapsed, leaving her critically incapacitated and in urgent need of rescue. Meanwhile, unseen beyond the colony's borders, a transport pod carrying a crash-landed enemy farmhand hurtled toward the surface.
